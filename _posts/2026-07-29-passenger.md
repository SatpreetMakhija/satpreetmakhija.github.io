Let melancholy pass through you,<br>
for nothing transpired.<br>
Watch bees grow fat in nearby nectar.<br>
Let hammering nails next door know no bother.<br>
Leave each moment; you do not return.<br>
Let sunshine warm you up like a caramel cookie,<br>
each whiff carrying you home.<br>
Let judgement be a day of the past.<br>
The ecstasy that awaits knows no bounds.<br>
Let you be lost –<br>
in what came and what comes,<br>
And you watch in what is this distant dance.<br>
Drown in the starry night,<br>
with madira by your side,<br>
and watch, and watch, and watch,<br>
before your eyes bid goodbye.<br>
Enter the God that you lost.
