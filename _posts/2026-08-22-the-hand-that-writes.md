I write of my crimes with my right hand.<br>
The already repenting angel sins while the demon watches.<br>
I wish I had trained my left hand to write.<br>
At least confess the crimes you're responsible for.<br>
And so the angel sins in ink,<br>
while the demon keeps its hands clean.
