Try till the moment you achieve.<br>
History made in the sands of time you leave.<br>
Win may not be consistent.<br>
But, you must be.<br>
To try, to oneself don't lie.<br>
Times may come.<br>
Hard, soft, rough and smooth.<br>
Looks like you may be screwed.<br>
But a way lies beneath.<br>
Deepen through the roots of your heart.<br>
Move the wheels of your life bound cart.<br>
To try is not to win.<br>
But, to lit up even if it is dim.<br>
To fall is not to lose.<br>
But, keep your head held high.<br>
Get up even stronger.<br>
To try even longer.<br>
Try till the moment you achieve.<br>
History made in the sands of time you leave.
