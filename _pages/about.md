Hey, I'm Satpreet. I build AI safety products at [Truth Audit Labs](https://truthauditlabs.ai/). Some believe AGI is already here. I'm starting to join that camp, but it's not safe in its current form, and that's one of the biggest problems to solve right now.

Before switching to AI safety, I worked on building a programming language for causality using formal methods at the [Vachani School of Advanced Computing](https://www.ashoka.edu.in/vachani-school-of-advanced-computing/). I learned my engineering chops at [Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/distributed-cloud-air-gapped) building portable data centers. It turns out air-gapped software is really complicated. I have a bachelor's degree in computer science from [Ashoka University](https://www.ashoka.edu.in/), which has helped me develop a liberal approach to problem solving.

Say hi if we're into the same things!
