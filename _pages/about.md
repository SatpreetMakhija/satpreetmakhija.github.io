I'm a software engineer and researcher with a background in distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, and platform engineering. I'm currently a researcher at the [Vachani School of Advanced Computing](https://www.ashoka.edu.in/vachani-school-of-advanced-computing/), working on programming languages and formal methods. Previously at [Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/distributed-cloud) and [Firebolt](https://www.firebolt.io/), I built Kubernetes-based infrastructure and software for air-gapped environments.
